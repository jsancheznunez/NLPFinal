# Evaluation: VADER sentiment vs. star ratings

Generated by `python scripts/evaluate.py` from `data/sample/reviews.jsonl` (11,233 Grocery_and_Gourmet_Food reviews). Re-run it after changing `analyzer/nltk_layer.py`.

## Setup
- **Prediction:** NLTK 3.9.4 VADER `compound` on the review text, through `analyzer/nltk_layer.py` (the same code the build and the browser run). Labels: positive >= 0.05, negative <= -0.05, otherwise neutral (VADER README thresholds).
- **Weak gold labels from stars:** 1-2 stars = negative, 3 = neutral, 4-5 = positive. Stars are a *proxy* for the sentiment of the text, not a human sentiment annotation.
- **Headline metric: macro-F1**, because the classes are imbalanced: 1 star 1,156, 2 star 577, 3 star 621, 4 star 1,010, 5 star 7,869.

| Accuracy | 0.791 |

| Macro-F1, binary (positive vs. negative only, 9,750 reviews) | 0.804 |

|---|---|---|---|

## Reading the numbers
- VADER is strong on clear praise: positive recall is 88.9%.
- It is weaker on complaints: only 53.4% of 1-2 star reviews are scored negative. Many complaints are factual descriptions of a defect or a return, with few lexicon words.
- The neutral class is the hardest (F1 0.095): 3-star reviews are usually mixed ("tasty, but too salty"), and VADER sums them to a clearly positive or negative compound.
- The small domain lexicon in `nltk_layer.DOMAIN_LEXICON` (e.g. stale, bland, expired, tasty; never overriding VADER's own ratings) changes macro-F1 from 0.507 to 0.522.

5-star reviews scored negative (strongest first):

|---|---|---|
| 1 | +0.9706 | I bought the honey mustard from the same brand and absolutely love it it’s so good. This honey bbq sauce tastes like robitusson or some kind of cough syrup yuck! I want to find a great sugar free bbq and wonder if the regular flavor tastes better |
| 1 | +0.9136 | I don't know what they did to these cookies but they have lost all flavor. I used to love these cookies. They were a holliday tradition in my house growing up. These are far from those cookies I used to love. |
| 1 | +0.9032 | I was very excited for the coffee since it had good reviews. Pretty much had to throw away more than half of the pods. They came ripped and coffee was everywhere. And this item is non returnable. Spent money for nothing. Very disappointed!! |
| 1 | +0.8999 | I have been dairy free for a long time and we all know there are some really great dairy free products out there. This is not one of them. I took one bite and threw the rest away. |
| 1 | +0.8911 | Flavor is not good. Almost taste like dirt to me. The heat level is well not there but I like the super “hot” stuff like death sauce from Blair’s. The thickness was perfect. |

Typical causes: negative words describing the *problem the product solved* ("best stuff to kill ants"), nostalgia or comparison with a better product ("I used to love these cookies", "I love the honey mustard... this bbq sauce tastes like cough syrup"), disappointment after positive expectations ("I was very excited... very disappointed"), domain phrases VADER does not know ("taste like dirt", "lost all flavor"), and reviews where the stars disagree with the text.

## NLP correctness review (spaCy + NLTK)
- **Shared code:** the playground imports the same `nltk_layer.py`. The Playwright test `tests/e2e/test_site.py::test_playground_matches_build_scores` checks that Pyodide 314.0.8 returns the same compound as the build (|diff| <= 1e-4) for the examples in `site/data/nltk_examples.json`.
- **Stopwords:** removed only for FreqDist and collocations, never before VADER. Negations stay in the vocabulary, so complaint phrases such as "not worth", "not work" and "stop work" appear among the negative collocations.
- **Collocations:** bigrams are counted inside sentences (`from_documents`), with a frequency filter (5 for the category, 3 per product) and the likelihood-ratio measure, so rare pairs do not dominate as they do with PMI.
- **Aspects:** the spaCy rules (amod, acomp + nsubj, opinion verb + dobj, conj, neg) are checked on golden sentences in `tests/test_spacy_layer.py`. Known gaps: the small model mislabels some entities (e.g. "polish" as NORP, "Norelco" as GPE), and adjectives that describe a type rather than an opinion are only partly filtered (`NON_OPINION_ADJ`).
- **Not done:** a hand-labeled set of about 100 aspect-opinion triples to measure extraction precision (and inter-annotator kappa). That needs human annotators, so it is listed as future work.

## Limitations
- VADER is a lexicon and rule model "specifically attuned to sentiments expressed in social media" (VADER README). It handles negation, boosters, capitals, punctuation and emoji, but not sarcasm or most domain-specific phrasing.
- Star ratings are a weak proxy for the sentiment of the text.
- "Sales" metrics are proxies (rating counts and review velocity), not revenue. No simulated sales data is used.
- The sample (200 products, up to 60 reviews each) over-represents popular products by design.
- spaCy analysis runs only at build time. Live analysis in the browser uses NLTK alone.
- Amazon Reviews 2023 declares no license, so it is used for academic, non-commercial work only (Hou et al. 2024, arXiv:2403.03952).

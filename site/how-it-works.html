How it works

Everything is computed on Netlify at build time, except the playground, which runs NLTK in your browser.

Results are labelled by the library that produced them: NLTK spaCy spaCy + NLTK Metrics (proxy)

Pipeline

  1. Data. A committed sample of Amazon Reviews 2023 (All_Beauty): reviews and metadata joined on parent_asin (data/sample/).
  2. spaCy en_core_web_sm 3.8.0 runs once over every review with nlp.pipe: lemmas, sentences, named entities (plus an EntityRuler placed before ner with BRAND/PRODUCT phrase patterns from the metadata), noun chunks, and dependency rules (amod; acomp + nsubj; opinion verb + dobj; conj; neg) that yield (aspect, opinion, negated) pairs.
  3. NLTK 3.9.4: VADER scores each review and each aspect opinion (positive ≥ 0.05, negative ≤ −0.05); FreqDist counts spaCy lemmas; bigram collocations are ranked by likelihood ratio with a frequency filter, separately for positive and negative reviews. The stopword list is NLTK English minus negations plus ecommerce noise words.
  4. Metrics (proxy) pandas computes the best-seller proxy, Bayesian rating, review velocity, the performance score and opportunity flags.
  5. Every JSON file is validated against schemas/*.schema.json and written to site/data/; Netlify publishes site/.

Performance score and opportunity flags Metrics (proxy)

There are no sales figures in the dataset. "Best-seller" means most-rated (rating_number), a proxy.

  • Bayesian rating = (v·R + m·C) / (v + m), with v reviews, product mean R, category mean C, m = 20.
  • Review velocity = (reviews in the last 6 months + 1) / (reviews in the 6 months before + 1), relative to the latest review date in the sample.
  • Score = 0.35·z(log(1 + rating_number)) + 0.25·z(Bayesian rating) + 0.25·z(mean VADER compound) + 0.15·z(log velocity).
  • Fix the top complaint: top 25% popularity but bottom 25% sentiment or adjusted rating. Under-marketed gem: top 25% sentiment, below-median popularity. Trending: velocity ≥ 1.5 with 5+ recent reviews in the sample. Price/value mismatch: 3+ negative price/value aspect or MONEY mentions.

NLP final project. NLTK (VADER, FreqDist, collocations) runs at build time and in your browser via Pyodide; spaCy (NER, EntityRuler, noun chunks, dependency aspects) runs only in the Netlify build. Sales figures are proxies (rating counts, review velocity); no simulated data is used.

Data: Amazon Reviews 2023, All_Beauty sample. Hou, Y., Li, J., He, Z., Yan, A., Chen, X., & McAuley, J. (2024). Bridging Language and Items for Retrieval and Recommendation. arXiv:2403.03952. No license is declared; used for academic, non-commercial purposes only.
